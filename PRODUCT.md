# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people commissioning a videographer and photographer (brand and marketing managers, music artists and their managers, couples planning a wedding, fashion labels and editors). They are judging taste and craft from the work itself, usually on a phone or laptop, often after a referral or an Instagram link.

Secondary: Evotekra prospects. This site is an Evotekra demo template shown to real videographers and photographers as an example of the portfolio site Evotekra can build for them.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio site for a single videographer and photographer that sells their work to people about to hire them. Success: a visitor watches the work, trusts the eye behind it, and makes contact.

## Positioning

One person who shoots both moving image and stills across four kinds of work: brand films and ads, music videos and concerts, weddings and events, fashion and portrait photography.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis. No build step. Content lives in `content.js`.
- Full motion is required: the user explicitly wants real motion, not a static page.
- Footage plays muted and inline; the showreel and films open in a full-screen player.
- Dark cinematic theme for this site (user's choice, 21 Nov 2026), an exception to their usual white-background preference.

## Brand Commitments

- The person is fictional: "Ireti Cole" is a placeholder name for the demo. Swappable.
- No real client names may be implied; credits must read as illustrative.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real portfolio footage yet. Stand-in stock footage and photos are used until a real client's work replaces them (user's decision, 21 Nov 2026).
- No testimonials, awards, press or client logos exist. Do not invent them as fact.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads. Interface recedes behind footage and photographs.
2. Every genre gets a fair showing, so any of the four buyer types finds their kind of work fast.
3. Motion serves the footage (reveal, pacing, cut), never decoration.
4. One clear way to get in touch.
